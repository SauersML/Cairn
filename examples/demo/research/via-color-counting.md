target: mutilated-board-untileable

Suppose a tiling by 31 dominoes existed. By domino-color-balance the
tiling would cover exactly 31 dark and 31 light squares. By
mutilated-board-color-count the region has 32 of one color and 30 of
the other. 31 ≠ 32, contradiction.
