title: The quadrant split preserves the induction hypothesis
---

Split the 2^n × 2^n board into four quadrants and place one L-tromino at
the center covering one cell of each quadrant that does not contain the
removed square. Then each quadrant is a 2^(n-1) × 2^(n-1) instance with
exactly one cell missing. (Open in this demo: state and prove this
carefully.)
